# Privacy

url_slice runs locally in your browser and only reads the active tab URL when you open or use the extension.

The extension uses browser history access to suggest previously visited URL segments and query values for editing. This
data stays on your device and is not sold, shared, transmitted, or used for advertising or analytics.

url_slice does not collect personal information, does not track browsing activity outside its URL editing features, and
does not send URL or history data to external servers.
